How it works

From payment to access.

The download can be shared, but Sleep Client only works with a valid product key linked to the buyer's Minecraft account.

01

Pay with DonutSMP money

Choose Monthly for 10M or Lifetime for 25M and send the amount in DonutSMP.

02

Payment gets verified

After the payment is confirmed, a product key is created for your Minecraft username.

03

Download Sleep Client

You receive access to the Sleep Client download. Sharing the download alone does not unlock the client.

04

Activate with your key

Enter your Minecraft username and product key. The license server checks that the key is valid and belongs to you.

05

Use Sleep Client

After successful activation, the client is unlocked for your account. Monthly access expires after 30 days, Lifetime does not expire.

✓

Protected access

One key is tied to one Minecraft account. A copied download file does not give another player a valid license.
